# Demo video script (2:50, under the 3:00 limit)

Rules that shape it: under 3 minutes; shows the project working on screen; public on YouTube; no copyrighted music.
Record the deployed site at 1440x900, browser zoom 110%. Speak calmly and say numbers exactly as they appear on screen.
The assistant's answers can vary between runs; never edit the footage to change what it said.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00-0:15 | Home page, then scroll to the Lightspeed study; hold on the source line. | "When you ask an AI assistant for something to buy, it usually recommends a brand it already knows. A study this fall found assistants picked the big chain about nine times out of ten. Small shops that ship on time rarely get a look." |
| 2 | 0:15-0:28 | Back to the top: the two answers to the same request. | "Real Deal Seal changes that with proof. We turn a shop's PayPal sales history into a signed passport that any AI assistant can read and check." |

Every fact says where it came from: PayPal's records, the shop itself, or simulated for this demo." |
